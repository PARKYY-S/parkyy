# PARKYY demo - 75 seconds, shot by shot

**Goal:** a hiring manager thinks "this person ships real, safe AI products." Show *work*, not music.
**Format:** screen recording 1080p, face cam optional (small, bottom corner), your voice-over (or captions).
**Tools:** OBS or Windows Game Bar (Win+G). Export MP4 for YouTube/LinkedIn + a 10-12 s GIF for the README.

## Before you hit record (2 minutes)
- Restart PARKYY. Make sure the pet roams and "Brain" chip is green (a cloud brain, not "starting...").
- Close personal tabs/windows. Hide chat history with private stuff. Use a plain wallpaper if you can.
- Do the task once off-camera so you know it works and the brain isn't rate-limited.
- Pick ONE task from "Work tasks" below. Don't show your real inbox or real contacts.

## Shot list
| Time | On screen | You say (or caption) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:08 | Desktop. PARKYY walks along a window, hops to another. | "This is PARKYY - my own AI assistant. Not another chatbot. It lives on my desktop." |
| 0:08-0:30 | Press Ctrl+Space. Say or type: *"Find the 3 biggest files in my Downloads and save me a note about them."* PARKYY plans it as a mission with steps. | "I ask it for real work. It turns it into a mission with steps instead of just replying." |
| 0:30-0:48 | The approval prompt appears (Yes/No with the action and its risk level). Click **Yes**. | "Here's the difference: it **asks me first** and shows exactly what it will do. Nothing happens without my Yes." |
| 0:48-1:00 | Open the saved note in the notes folder; show the mission's steps ticked. | "Then it **checks the real result** - the note exists, the steps are done - instead of trusting what the AI says." |
| 1:00-1:10 | Turn Wi-Fi off (or show the USB stick). Ask a quick question; answer comes from the offline brain. Show "[Offline Hermes]". | "No internet? It runs from a USB stick on a local model and syncs memory back." |
| 1:10-1:15 | Bench report. PARKYY waves. | "Built at zero budget, with its own benchmark and hidden tests. I'm Saud - links below." |

## Work tasks (pick one that works reliably on your setup)
1. **Biggest files + note (use this one):** the example above. It's the mission your benchmark already passes, so it's proven.
2. **Draft + schedule:** email + calendar. Only after Gmail & Calendar pass PARKYY's self-test (on 1 Nov it was the 1 failing check of 21) - re-connect Google with CONNECT-GOOGLE.bat and test it first.
3. **Research + brief:** "/web what changed in X this week, 5 bullets with sources" on the USB edition.

## Make it land
- Say the safety line out loud: **"It can't act without my Yes, and it checks its own work."** That is your hook.
- Keep it under 90 seconds. Cut every pause. Add captions (most people watch muted).
- Post: video + README link. Title: "I built a free, offline-capable AI assistant that asks before it acts."
- In your pitch to companies, say what you'd build for them: **safe, verifiable AI agents.**
